Jarvis Clipper

A personal desktop tool that turns long gaming livestreams into short vertical clips - and lets its owner review every clip before it is shared.

What it does

  1. Finds the most-watched moments of a livestream from public signals (viewer-made clips and chat activity).
  2. Cuts each moment into a short 9:16 video with captions and a credit to the streamer.
  3. Shows every clip to its owner, who approves, edits or rejects it.
  4. Approved clips are uploaded to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API - as a draft in the owner's TikTok inbox. Nothing is published until the owner opens TikTok and posts it.

Who it is for

Jarvis Clipper runs on one person's computer and is used only by its owner, with their own TikTok account. It is not a public service and has no user sign-ups.

TikTok data

The app asks TikTok only for basic profile information (user.info.basic) and permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload). See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
