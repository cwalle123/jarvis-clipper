Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Jarvis Clipper ("the app") is a personal desktop application used only by its owner on their own computer. This policy explains what data the app handles when it is connected to TikTok.

Data the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): the account's open ID, display name and avatar, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after the owner logs in, used only to upload approved videos.

What the app does with it

  • Uploads videos the owner has approved to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts (video.upload).
  • Tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer. They are never sent anywhere except back to TikTok.
  • The app does not collect data about other TikTok users, does not run analytics or ads, and does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

Video content

Clips are made from public livestreams and are stored on the owner's computer until posted; local copies are deleted automatically a few days after posting.

Removing access and data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, which revokes its tokens. Deleting the local token file removes all TikTok data the app holds.
